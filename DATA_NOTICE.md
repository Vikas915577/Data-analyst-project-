# Sample data notice

`synthetic_loans.csv` is fully synthetic and generated deterministically by `src/generate_demo_data.py`. It does **not** contain real LendingClub borrowers, and its default patterns are deliberately simulated to support testing and demonstration.

Use the public-data preparation path in `src/prepare_lendingclub.py` to process a separately obtained, permitted LendingClub historical CSV. Do not report synthetic segment rates as real findings, and do not upload borrower-level source records to a public repository.
