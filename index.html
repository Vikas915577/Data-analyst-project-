What it demonstrates

Reproducible sample generation, segmentation, portfolio metrics, temporal evaluation, and common classification metrics.

What it does not claim

Synthetic metrics do not verify real-world default rates. Segment differences are not causal. The model is not suitable for real lending decisions.
